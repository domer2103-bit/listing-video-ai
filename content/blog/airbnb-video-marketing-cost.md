# How Much Does Airbnb Video Marketing Cost? A Price Breakdown

Cost is usually the first question hosts ask about video, and the honest answer is that it varies enormously depending on the approach — from effectively free to several hundred pounds per property. Here's what actually drives that range.

## Option 1: Hiring a videographer

A professional videographer shooting and editing a walkthrough for a single Airbnb listing typically costs somewhere in the low hundreds of pounds, depending on property size, location, and how much editing (colour grading, music, motion graphics) is included. For a host with one property, this is usually a one-off cost, repeated only when the space changes significantly.

The economics get harder for hosts with multiple properties — repeating that cost across five or ten listings adds up quickly, and coordinating shoot schedules across several properties adds real time overhead on top of the direct cost.

## Option 2: DIY filming and editing

Filming a walkthrough yourself with a phone and editing it in a free or low-cost app removes the direct cash cost, but shifts the cost to time and skill. A polished-looking DIY video — steady shots, decent pacing, clean cuts, maybe some narration or captions — realistically takes several hours to film and edit properly, and the skill gap between a first attempt and something genuinely competitive with professional video is real.

For hosts with the time and inclination to learn video editing, this can work well. For most hosts running a property alongside other work, the time cost tends to be the actual barrier, not the cash cost.

## Option 3: AI-generated video from existing photos

This is where the economics shift most. Tools like [Online Viewing](https://www.onlineviewing.co.uk) generate a fully narrated, animated video directly from photos you already have, with pricing typically structured as:

- **A free tier** — usually one video, enough to fully evaluate the output on a real listing.
- **Entry paid tiers** (roughly £15–£25/month in most tools' pricing) — covering several videos a month, suitable for a host with one or two properties needing occasional refreshes.
- **Mid tiers** (roughly £40–£60/month) — more videos per month, a fuller voice library, and often production extras like an AI-generated establishing shot.
- **Top tiers** (£100+/month) — built for hosts or property managers running many listings, with higher video allowances and batch generation.

Compared to hiring a videographer per property, the per-video cost at any paid tier is typically a fraction of a single filmed walkthrough — and for a host managing several properties, one monthly subscription covers all of them rather than requiring a separate booking and payment per property.

## What actually drives the cost comparison

The real comparison isn't "free vs paid" — it's cost per video across each approach:

| Approach | Typical cost | Time to produce |
|---|---|---|
| Professional videographer | £150–£400+ per property | Days (scheduling + editing) |
| DIY filming and editing | £0 cash, several hours of time | Hours per video |
| AI-generated from photos | Free–£150/month, covering multiple properties | Minutes per video |

For a single-property host doing this once, a videographer or DIY approach might make sense depending on available time and budget. For a host with multiple properties, or one who wants to refresh video regularly rather than as a one-off, the AI approach's per-video economics are difficult for either alternative to match.

## Thinking about return, not just cost

The relevant question isn't just what a video costs, but what it costs relative to what it returns. If video meaningfully improves a listing's booking rate — even a modest uplift — the additional revenue from filled nights that would otherwise have sat empty typically outweighs the cost of producing the video many times over, at any of these price points. This is part of why video has shifted from a luxury add-on to something increasingly treated as standard listing maintenance, the same way professional photography did a decade earlier.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it worth paying for video if I only have one Airbnb property?**
Often yes, even at one property — the free tier of most AI video tools covers exactly this case, and even a paid entry tier tends to be inexpensive relative to a single professional video shoot.

**Do I need to keep paying monthly, or can I generate a video once and stop?**
Most tools allow generating videos without an ongoing subscription commitment at lower tiers, though a monthly plan makes more sense if you're managing multiple properties or refreshing video regularly.

**Does a more expensive tier actually produce a better-quality video?**
Generally the core video quality is similar across tiers — higher tiers add volume, voice options, and extras like establishing shots rather than a fundamentally different core product.

## The bottom line

Video no longer requires a several-hundred-pound production budget per property. For most hosts, particularly those managing more than one listing, AI-generated video from existing photos offers a materially better cost-per-video than either hiring a videographer or investing the time to DIY it — often at a fraction of the cost of a single filmed walkthrough.
