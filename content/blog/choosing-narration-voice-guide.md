# Choosing a Narration Voice for Your Property Video: A Quick Guide

Most AI video tools offer a choice of narration voices, and the right choice genuinely does affect how a video lands — a mismatch between voice tone and property type can undercut an otherwise well-produced video. Here's a practical way to think about the choice.

## Start with the property type, not personal preference

It's tempting to pick whichever voice simply sounds best in isolation, but the more useful question is which voice fits the specific property and its likely audience. A warm, conversational voice that suits a family home might feel oddly casual for a high-end city apartment aimed at investors; a formal, polished voice that suits a luxury listing might feel stiff and impersonal for a cosy starter home.

## A rough matching framework

**Family homes and starter properties:** A warmer, more conversational tone generally fits — narration that sounds like a knowledgeable friend rather than a formal presenter tends to land well with buyers imagining a life in the space, not just evaluating specifications.

**High-end or luxury listings:** A more polished, measured tone tends to suit the register buyers expect at this price point — unhurried pacing and precise language over an overly casual or enthusiastic delivery.

**Airbnb and short-term rentals:** Often benefits from a friendlier, slightly more energetic tone than a home sale — the decision being made is different (a short stay, not a life decision), and the narration can reflect that lighter context.

**Commercial or investment properties:** Usually suits a neutral, factual tone that prioritises clarity over warmth — buyers and investors at this stage are typically evaluating numbers and specifications more than emotional fit.

## Gender and accent considerations

Beyond tone, voice gender and accent are worth considering relative to your likely audience, though there's no universally "correct" choice — testing a sample against your specific listing and target buyer is more reliable than a general rule. If you're marketing internationally or to a specific demographic, an accent that feels locally familiar to that audience can help, though a neutral, clear accent is a safe default when audience is uncertain.

## Consistency across a portfolio

If you're managing multiple listings — as a host with several properties, or an agent with a full pipeline — using the same voice consistently across your video content can help build a recognisable, professional feel across your portfolio, similar to consistent branding. This is worth weighing against the case-by-case matching above: for most, picking one strong, versatile default voice and using it consistently is simpler and works well enough across varied property types.

## What to check once you've chosen

Before finalising, listen for a few specific things: does the pacing feel natural rather than rushed or overly slow, does the voice pronounce any local place names or unusual terms correctly, and does the overall tone match how you'd actually want to describe the property if you were doing it yourself. If something feels off, most tools let you regenerate with a different voice quickly rather than being locked into the first choice.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I preview a voice before committing to a full video generation?**
Most tools offer a short sample or preview before generating the full narration — it's worth using this rather than judging a voice from a generic sample elsewhere.

**Is there a "safest" default voice choice if I'm unsure?**
A warm but professional, moderately paced voice tends to work reasonably well across most property types as a default, if you'd rather not overthink the choice for every listing.

**Can I change the voice after a video is already generated?**
Typically yes, by regenerating with a different voice selection — this usually doesn't require re-uploading photos or re-entering property details.

## The bottom line

Voice choice isn't a minor cosmetic detail — the right tone, matched to property type and audience, measurably affects how well a video lands. A few minutes spent choosing deliberately, rather than defaulting to whatever's first in the list, is worth the effort.
