# AI-Narrated Video Explained: How Online Viewing Turns Photos Into Tours

It's a reasonable question to ask how this actually works before trusting it with a listing. Here's a plain-language explanation of what happens between uploading photos and getting a finished video back.

## The starting material: your photos

The process starts with the photos you already have — either uploaded directly, or pulled automatically from a Rightmove listing URL. Nothing needs to be reshot or specially prepared; the same photos already published on Zoopla, Airbnb, or anywhere else work just as well, just via direct upload instead of a URL.

## Step 1: Sequencing

The tool analyses the photo set and arranges it into a logical walkthrough order — typically an exterior or entrance shot first, then a sensible room-by-room sequence, rather than the order the photos happen to be uploaded in. This mimics how a person would actually move through a property in person.

## Step 2: Motion

Each still photo is animated with camera-style movement — a slow pan, a gentle zoom toward a focal point in the room — rather than simply displayed as a static frame. This is what turns a photo slideshow into something that reads as a continuous tour rather than a sequence of stills.

## Step 3: Narration

Based on the details provided during upload (property type, location, key features, any facts worth mentioning), the tool generates a script and turns it into spoken narration using AI voice synthesis. This is where the "AI-narrated" part of the process happens — a natural-sounding voice describing what's on screen, timed to match the visuals.

## Step 4: Pacing and scoring

The finished sequence is assembled with pacing that reflects the relative importance of each space, plus background music, to produce something that feels like a coherent finished video rather than a mechanically stitched-together clip.

## What the AI is actually doing, and what it isn't

It's worth being precise about this: the AI is generating motion, narration and sequencing from real photos you provide — it is not fabricating rooms, features, or details that don't exist in the source material. The video reflects what's actually in your photos; it doesn't invent content beyond what's given to it. This distinction matters both for accuracy (a video shouldn't show something the property doesn't have) and for trust (guests and buyers are evaluating a video that represents the actual property).

## Why this matters for accuracy

Because the video is built from your actual photos and the details you provide, its accuracy depends on those inputs — outdated photos will produce a video of the property as it looked then, and any facts entered incorrectly will appear in the narration as given. This is worth double-checking during the review step of the process, the same way you'd proofread any other listing content.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the AI create new images, or only animate existing ones?**
It animates and sequences your existing photos — it isn't generating new visual content beyond what's in your source images.

**How is the narration voice generated?**
Through AI voice synthesis based on a script generated from the details you provide about the property — most tools offer a choice of voice styles.

**Can I edit the narration script before it's finalised?**
This varies by tool and plan — some allow reviewing or adjusting details and regenerating; check what your specific plan supports.

## The bottom line

AI-narrated video isn't a black box — it's a fairly explainable pipeline of sequencing, motion, narration and pacing, applied to photos you already have. Understanding the mechanism makes it easier to trust the output and to know what to check during review.
