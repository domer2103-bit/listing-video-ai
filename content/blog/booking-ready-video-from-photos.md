# How to Create a Booking-Ready Video From Your Existing Airbnb Photos

If your Airbnb listing already has a decent photo set, you're closer to having a video than you might think. This is the actual step-by-step process, using the photos already sitting in your listing — no reshoot, no video equipment, no editing software.

## Step 1: Start from your existing listing

Before uploading anything new, check whether your video tool can pull directly from your live Airbnb listing URL. Tools like [Online Viewing](https://www.onlineviewing.co.uk) can often generate a video straight from a pasted listing link, using the photos already published there — which means the fastest path to a first video is usually your listing as it already exists, not a fresh upload.

If you'd rather use a specific photo set (an updated selection, or photos not yet published to the listing), uploading them directly works the same way.

## Step 2: Fill in what the photos alone don't convey

A short assistant-style intake typically asks for the handful of details a photo set can't answer on its own:

- **The property type and location** — "2-bed flat in Shoreditch" gives the tool context photos alone don't provide.
- **The nightly rate**, if you want it referenced.
- **Which photo is which room**, if it's ambiguous — this matters more for open-plan spaces or unusual layouts.
- **Which narration voice** you'd like, if the platform offers a choice.

This step usually takes under a minute, and it's the main thing separating a generic-sounding video from one that actually reflects your specific property.

## Step 3: Let it generate

From here, the tool handles the actual work — animating each photo with appropriate motion, sequencing rooms into a logical order, generating narration from the details provided, and scoring the whole thing with music and pacing. This typically takes about two minutes for a standard property.

## Step 4: Review before publishing

Watch the finished video once before adding it to your listing. A few things worth checking specifically:

- **Does the room order make sense** as a walkthrough, or does it jump around in a way a real visitor wouldn't experience?
- **Is anything mislabelled** — a bedroom described as a living room, for instance, if the intake step got a detail wrong?
- **Does the pacing feel right**, or does any single room get disproportionately more or less time than it deserves?

Most issues at this stage are quick fixes — adjusting a label or a detail and regenerating, rather than starting over.

## Step 5: Publish it everywhere it'll help

Once you're happy with it:

- **Add it to your Airbnb listing** through the platform's standard media upload.
- **Post it on Instagram or TikTok**, either the full version or a shorter cut, to drive awareness and direct bookings.
- **Include it in pre-arrival messaging** to confirmed guests, which helps set accurate expectations before check-in.
- **Reuse it across other platforms** if you list the same property on more than one (Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com), since the same video generally works across all of them.

## Keeping the video current

A video reflects the property as it looked when the photos were taken. If you renovate a room, change furniture significantly, or notice the current video no longer matches reality, it's worth regenerating rather than leaving an outdated video live — the expectation-mismatch problem that hurts photo-only listings applies just as much to video that's fallen out of date.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to redo the video for each platform I list on (Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com)?**
No — the same video generally works across platforms, as long as each supports video in its listing format. Check each platform's specific upload requirements.

**How often should I update my listing video?**
Whenever your photos change meaningfully — after a renovation, a significant furniture update, or if the current video is more than a year or two old and no longer reflects the space accurately.

**Can I generate a shorter version for social media separately from the full listing video?**
Yes — most tools support generating or trimming a shorter cut specifically for platforms like Instagram or TikTok, where shorter, faster-paced content performs better.
